One sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday
annoyance, for the **u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a**
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun) ($2.50 USDC, free to enter, judged
pairwise by a house model on how funny, clever and original the pitch is).

## The pitch

> The Self-Untangling Headphones: pulled from your pocket, they perform a tiny interpretive dance until every knot unties itself in applause.

139 characters, one sentence. The laugh is the invention itself — headphones
doing an interpretive dance, knots applauding as they untie — and the absurd
machine genuinely fixes the annoyance: dancing untangles the knots. No invented
numbers, no overclaim. Full review of 11 candidates over 3 rounds: see
`REVIEW.md`.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the pitch
to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Run twice, the output is byte-identical (sha256
`cbf5285abaa0af2acf28ed6a91342ae4e04b27e697704a7cb7a269ec1beade44`).
